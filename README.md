# HR Attrition Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes employee attrition patterns and workforce demographics to help HR teams identify key factors contributing to employee turnover and improve retention strategies.

## Business Problem

Employee attrition can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and impact organizational performance. The goal of this dashboard is to identify trends and patterns behind employee turnover.

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Excel
* DAX
* Data Visualization

## Key KPIs

* Total Employees: 1470
* Total Attrition: 237
* Attrition Rate: 16.12%
* Active Employees: 1233
* Average Age: 37
* Average Monthly Income: 6.5K

## Dashboard Insights

* Employees aged 25–34 contribute the highest attrition count.
* Sales Representatives have the highest attrition rate among job roles.
* Employees working overtime are more likely to leave the organization.
* Life Sciences employees show the highest attrition count among education fields.
* R&D department contributes the largest share of attrition.
